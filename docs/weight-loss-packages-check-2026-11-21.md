# Weight-loss packages: what the base says — 2026-11-21

Read-only check of the three weight-loss items, asked before the clinic's Monday review.
Answers two questions: **does a patient come back?** and **does every visit carry a value?**

## How the clinic actually sold them

The package is not one 1.980,00 sale. It is **one charge per visit**, at whatever the patient
paid that day. The 495 is only the most common amount, not a rule: 69 of the 186 Tirzepatide
charges are something else.

## Does every visit carry a value? No — 24 do not.

- Sales with no money: **0**. Lines with no value: **0**. Every registered sale is worth
  something.
- But **24 show-up appointments** belong to these patients and have **no sale that day** (not
  even within a day either side). They are visits with no purchase, recorded through the
  agenda rather than a sale.

The 24 are the only visits without a value, so the gap is not in the amounts — it is in the
visits that were never charged.
